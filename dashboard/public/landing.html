How Nova AutoSecure Works

A frictionless 3-step pipeline engineered for sub-second precision.

1

Ingestion & Verification

Input targets via /secure (OTP, recovery code, secret key) or capture through high-conversion customizable verification funnels.

2

Instant Auto-Lockdown

In less than two seconds, 2FA is purged, Windows Hello keys removed, primary aliases updated, sessions revoked, and new passwords applied.

3

Inventory & Split Claim

Accounts are automatically checked for capes, Hypixel status, and billing. Assets are routed via configurable auto-split ratios to team members.
