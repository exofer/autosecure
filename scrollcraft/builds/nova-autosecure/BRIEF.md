## 7. Structure & Grammar Choice
- **Chosen Grammar:** **Chaptered Editorial** with **Dimensional Spatial Plates**.
- **Why others were dismissed:**
  - *Filmic One-Shot:* Rejected because it is the overused default of past builds and relies on heavy video scrub chains rather than bespoke spatial architectural plates.
  - *Continuous World:* Rejected because it forces a single uninterrupted 3D video flythrough rather than structured substantive editorial security chapters.
  - *Live Surface:* Rejected because this is the public-facing marketing sanctuary/portal introducing Nova Autosecure, not the internal operations dashboard itself.
  - *Typographic Poster:* Rejected because Nova Autosecure requires physical spatial rendering of the Aegis shield and dimensional account layers, not pure text.
  - *Gallery / Catalog:* Rejected because Nova is an integrated security system, not an eCommerce catalog of disparate physical items.
  - *Split Stage:* Compelling, but Chaptered Editorial allows a richer sacred manuscript / celestial folio cadence that suits the "heavenly" prompt.
  - *Rhythmic Cutlist:* Rejected because violent fast-paced cuts directly destroy the celestial, majestic calm required by the prompt.
